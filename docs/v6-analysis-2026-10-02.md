# Video, voice, games, 3D sites, and what every expensive feature should cost

Analysis only. Nothing in this round was built, and the design mockups were
not applied: the message that asked for them says `Διάλεξα: [θα το πεις]`
with the choice left blank.

## How to read a number in this document

Every figure is one of four things, and the tag says which:

- **MEASURED** — produced by a command in this repo, named beside it. Re-run
  it rather than trust it.
- **LOOKED UP 2026-10-02** — a provider's public price, with its source. These
  move; the date is the claim.
- **DERIVED** — arithmetic over the two above, with the inputs shown.
- **UNKNOWN** — not measured, not looked up. Written as a dash, never as a
  guess.

## 1. The margin is not 4x, and a gate demanding 4x would go red everywhere

    node scripts/measure-margin.mjs

**MEASURED 2026-10-02.** `CREDIT_MARGIN_MULTIPLIER` resolves to 4, and it
decides **0 of 150** (feature, plan) combinations. Every settlement uses the
plan multiplier, which is above the floor everywhere:

| | free | starter | growth | professional | ultimate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| multiplier actually applied | **6x** | **5x** | **5x** | **5x** | **5x** |

So "margin ΑΚΡΙΒΩΣ 4x παντού" is not a tightening of what exists — it is a
**reduction**, and a mutation written to fail when the margin is not 4 would
fail on all 150 combinations today.

### And 5x on paper is not 5x on revenue

`src/lib/billing/credit-formula.ts` carries the reason, and it is the thing to
settle before any new feature is priced. A credit's list price is EUR 0.02,
but every plan sells credits below that in bulk:

| plan | credits | price | EUR per credit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | 1,000 | EUR 20 | 0.0200 |
| Growth | 3,000 | EUR 50 | 0.0167 |
| Professional | 10,000 | EUR 100 | 0.0100 |
| Ultimate | 25,000 | EUR 200 | 0.0080 |

Charging `cost x M / 0.02` guarantees M against the **list** price, not
against what the customer paid. The file records the collapse: on Ultimate the
real multiple becomes `4 x (0.008 / 0.02) = 1.6x`, and the plan table before
the fix showed Growth 30%, Professional 50%, **Ultimate 62.5%** of revenue
going to cost against a 25% ceiling. The code now divides by the plan's own
rate, which is why `make-cost.mjs` shows the same website at 87 credits on
free and 180 on Ultimate.

**The decision you actually have to make:** the number to hold constant is the
margin **on revenue**, not the multiplier in the config. They are the same
number only on Starter.

## 1b. Two model prices in the table are wrong, and both over-charge

**MEASURED 2026-10-02** against the current published rates:

| id in `model-pricing.ts` | the table says | the rate is | effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| `claude-sonnet-4-6` | $3 / $15 | $3 / $15 | correct — and it is what every feature requests today |
| `claude-sonnet-5` | $3 / $15 | **$2 / $10** | over-estimates cost by 50% |
| `claude-opus-5-5` | **absent** | $4 / $20 | falls back to $10 / $50 — 2.5x |
| `claude-sonnet-5-5` | **absent** | $2 / $10 | falls back to $10 / $50 — 5x |

Nothing is broken today, because every AI feature in this app requests
`claude-sonnet-4-6` and that row is right. The file's own comment explains the
fallback and defends it: an unknown model is priced as the most expensive known
one, "the safe direction to fail in", and the comment names the incident that
rule was written after.

**But safe-for-margin is not the same as right.** The direction of the error
here is the customer's: the moment anyone bumps the model constant to a 5.5
model, every action is charged against a cost two-and-a-half to five times the
real one, silently, and the margin alert stays quiet because the stored margin
is computed from the same inflated figure — the exact mechanism the comment
above it describes, running the other way.

Fixing it is four lines. It is worth doing before the model constant moves, not
after.

## 2. Video

### Nothing is wired. No code reads any video provider.

**MEASURED 2026-10-02** (`grep` over `src/`): zero files reference RUNWAY,
LUMA, KLING, REPLICATE or FAL. The Videos screen is a record list. A Runway key
in the environment changes nothing today.

### What the providers actually sell

**LOOKED UP 2026-10-02.** Sources at the end.

| provider | resolution | per second | max per call |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kling 2.5 Turbo | 720p | $0.042 | 10s (extend to ~3 min total) |
| Kling 2.5 Turbo | 1080p | $0.07 | 10s |
| Kling 2.5 | 4K | $0.42 | 10s |
| Runway Gen-4 Turbo | 720p | $0.05 | 5s or 10s |
| Runway Gen-4.5 | 720p | $0.12 | 5s or 10s |
| Luma Ray3 | 1080p | ~$0.24 | 5s or 10s (Modify to ~20s) |

**The premise to correct first: nobody generates 30 seconds in one call.**
Every provider caps a single generation at 5 or 10 seconds. A 30-second video
is three to six clips stitched, and the seams are the quality problem, not the
cost. "Minutes" is Kling's extend chain only, and only to about 3 minutes.

**8K video does not exist from any of them. 4K exists on Kling and costs
ten times 720p.**

### What a 30-second video would cost, if it existed

**DERIVED** from the rates above, `usdToEurRate: 0.92` and the plan credit
prices, at the 5x the code applies:

| 30s at | API cost | credits on Growth | credits on Ultimate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kling 720p | $1.26 | ~347 | ~724 |
| Runway Gen-4 Turbo 720p | $1.50 | ~413 | ~863 |
| Luma 1080p | $7.20 | ~1,983 | ~4,140 |

Read the Free column that is missing: Free has **100 credits a month**. One
30-second video is between 3x and 20x a Free user's entire month, and a Luma
30-second clip is two thirds of a Growth month. **Video is not a feature that
needs a tier gate added to it; video is a feature that only exists above
Growth.**

### The generation wait, and what it needs that does not exist

2-10 minutes of generation is correct in shape, and it breaks the current
architecture: every AI route in this app is a request that holds a reservation
and settles in the same handler. A job that outlives its HTTP request needs a
jobs table, a worker, and a notification — `src/app/api/jobs` exists, and
whether it can carry this is **UNKNOWN** (not read in this round).

**Storage: UNKNOWN.** No measurement of what a generated clip weighs or what
the bucket costs was taken, and I will not guess it. It is a 20-minute
measurement once a single clip exists.

## 3. Voice

### Half of it is already built

**MEASURED 2026-10-02.** `src/lib/voice/voice-pricing.ts` is wired and real:

| | model | rate (in the code) |
|---|---|---|
| listening | `whisper-1` | $0.006 per minute |
| speaking | `eleven_turbo_v2_5` | $0.15 per 1,000 characters |

`MAX_CLIP_SECONDS = 120`. Minutes per plan: free 0, starter 30, growth 90,
professional 300, ultimate 900, enterprise 2000.

### Listening is cheap. Speaking is eighteen times more expensive.

**DERIVED** at 900 spoken characters per minute (the constant in the file),
5x, Growth rate:

| | API cost per minute | credits per minute (Growth) |
|---|---|---|
| transcribe | $0.006 | ~2 |
| speak | $0.135 | ~37 |

**This is the finding that matters for the feature you asked about.** "Speak a
command and it happens" only needs the cheap half. Whisper at 2 credits a
minute is close to free; it is the talking back that costs. A voice **command**
feature can ship without ElevenLabs at all.

### What is missing is not transcription, it is routing

The gap between "it transcribed you" and "it did the thing" is a router that
turns a sentence into a tool. `src/lib/ai/module-vocabulary.ts` does half of
it (13 modules, 10 languages, accent and final-sigma folding). **Its accuracy
has never been measured.** Until it is, "say it and it happens" is a design,
not a feature — and the same router is what the `c-simple` mockup's
`Going to [Build a site] change` chip depends on.

**This is the cheapest high-value thing on this page**: 50 real Greek and
English phrases through the existing router, one afternoon, and the number
decides whether voice commands and the new home screen are both buildable or
both blocked.

## 4. Images, 8K, and upscaling

### Nothing is wired here either

**MEASURED 2026-10-02:** zero files in `src/` read FLUX, IDEOGRAM, RECRAFT,
TOPAZ, MAGNIFIC or STABILITY as a generator. The only image source wired is
Unsplash (14 files) plus the user's own uploads.

### The honest answer about 8K: nobody sells it

**LOOKED UP 2026-10-02.** FLUX 1.1 Pro Ultra tops out at **4 megapixels** —
about 2048x2048, or 2752x1536 at 16:9. That is the ceiling of the premium tier,
and 8K (7680px wide, ~33 MP at 16:9) is **eight times more pixels than the best
model produces natively**.

| model | price per image | ceiling |
|---|---|---|
| Ideogram 3 | ~$0.03 | — |
| Recraft V4.1 | ~$0.035 | — |
| Flux 1.1 Pro Ultra | ~$0.06 | 4 MP |
| Recraft V4.1 Pro | ~$0.21 | — |

**DERIVED** at 5x on Growth: Ideogram ~9 credits, Recraft ~10, Flux Ultra ~17,
Recraft Pro ~63 per image.

### Upscaling is the only road to 8K, and it invents the detail

| upscaler | cost per image | note |
|---|---|---|
| Real-ESRGAN | $0 | open source, self-hosted, needs a GPU |
| Magnific | ~$0.05-0.20 | invents detail; good on AI art, wrong on photographs |
| Topaz Gigapixel | ~$0.30 amortised | subscription-only since Oct 2025 |

**Say this to the customer in those words, or do not offer it.** An upscaled
8K image is a 4 MP image with pixels invented by a second model. For an
illustration that is an improvement. For a photograph of someone's shopfront it
is a quiet fabrication, and this project has a rule against exactly that shape.

**My recommendation: 4 MP is the ceiling you advertise.** It is the real one.
Offer 8K as "enlarged", never as "generated", and only on Ultimate — Topaz at
~83 credits an image is a tier-gated action regardless.

**Storage per file: UNKNOWN** — no measurement taken.

## 5. Websites: the 10x you guessed is already measured, and already charged

This is the one place where the thing you asked for mostly exists.

**MEASURED**, recorded in `src/lib/billing/estimate.ts` lines 149-158 from a
real settlement run:

| | held | charged |
|---|---|---|
| a simple site | 22 | **26 credits** |
| a complex site | 61 | **264 credits** |

**That is 10.2x, measured, between a simple and a complex site — and it is
already what the user pays**, because settlement divides real token counts by
the same rate. Credits already scale with real cost.

What does **not** exist:

1. **The levels.** There is one website action, not three. Nothing in the UI
   says simple / premium / 3D, and nothing makes the model build differently.
2. **The number before the fact.** The estimate shown is one profile
   (`baseOutputChars: 34000`), so a complex site is reserved as if it were
   ordinary and the difference appears only at settlement. The screen cannot
   say "this one is 450 credits" because nothing knows yet which one it is.
3. **The tier gate.** `node scripts/make-cost.mjs` gives a plain site 87
   credits on Free, whose whole month is 100. A 10x site is already impossible
   on Free — by accident, through the balance, not by a rule that says so.

### Is a 3D site technically possible through this builder?

**MEASURED:** `WEBSITE_MAX_TOKENS = 128000`, with one initial call plus up to
four continuation rounds. The output ceiling is not the obstacle — 128k tokens
is roughly 500KB of HTML, and a Three.js + GSAP single-file site is well inside
it.

**Updated the same day, 2026-10-02 — see `docs/v6-3d-sites-2026-10-02.md`.**
Four of the five questions now have measured answers from a hand-written
sample run eight ways: the obstacle is **not** the output ceiling but
`stripDisallowedExternalScripts`, which removes every `<script src>`; 3D adds
about 2 credits on Growth, not 8-10x; the mobile fallback and SEO hold in every
scenario. Still **UNKNOWN**: whether the model reliably writes it, which needs
a key this environment does not have.

**The one measurement worth buying:** one 3D site, generated for real, about
EUR 1.00-1.50 of model time. It answers feasibility, cost multiple, load time
and mobile fallback in a single run. Everything in this section after that is
arithmetic.

### The moat holds in 3D, and that part is already built

`buildBusinessContextBlock` puts the person's own records into the website
prompt. A 3D site built through the same route gets the same records. **Framer
does 3D and does not know your products** — that sentence survives the upgrade,
and it costs nothing extra to keep, because the context block is upstream of
whatever the model is asked to produce.

## 6. Games

**No code, no provider, no measurement.** The honest analysis is short:

A browser game is an HTML artifact — the same shape the website builder
already emits, with the same 128k ceiling and roughly the same token cost.
Technically it is the cheapest thing on this page to build: a prompt change and
a new row.

**The question that has no answer is who asks for it.** This product sells to
people with invoices, customers and a shopfront. A quiz for a cafe's Instagram
is plausible; a platformer is not, and "the user plays" is a different product
with different retention economics. There are **0 users** to ask.

**Recommendation: not now, and not because it is hard.** It is the only item on
this page with no identified buyer, and shipping it would widen the surface the
17 tools already cover without answering a question anybody has asked.

## 8. What I would do, in this order

1. **Settle the margin question** (half a day, no new feature). Decide whether
   the invariant is 5x on revenue or 4x on something else, then gate the
   **value**, not its existence. Until that is settled, every number in section
   7 is provisional.
2. **Measure the router** (one afternoon, ~EUR 2). 50 phrases through
   `module-vocabulary`. One number that unblocks voice commands AND the new
   home screen's routed chip. Nothing below this is worth starting first.
3. **One real 3D site** (~EUR 1.50). Four of the five questions were answered
   on 2026-10-02 by a hand-written sample instead (`docs/v6-3d-sites-2026-10-02.md`);
   the real generation still answers the fifth, model reliability.
4. **Website levels + cost-before** (2-3 days). The 10x already happens; make
   it visible and gated. This is the highest-value build on the page because
   the expensive case already exists and is currently a surprise.
5. **Video** (a week, and it needs the background-job machinery first). Not
   before you want to sell a Growth-and-up feature.
6. **Images at 4 MP** (2 days). Cheap, well-understood, no architecture needed.
7. **8K** — offer as "enlarged", Ultimate only, or not at all.
8. **Games** — no.
