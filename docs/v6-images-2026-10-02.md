# Images at the highest quality — 2K, 4K, 8K, and what is true about each

Analysis only, written 2026-10-02. Nothing was built and no image provider is
wired: **MEASURED 2026-10-02**, zero files in `src/` call FLUX, Ideogram,
Recraft, Seedream, Gemini image, Topaz or Magnific. The only image sources
today are Unsplash and the person's own uploads.

Tags as in `docs/v6-analysis-2026-10-02.md`: **MEASURED**, **LOOKED UP**
(dated), **DERIVED** (inputs shown), or a dash.

## 0. A correction to my own earlier sentence

`docs/v6-analysis-2026-10-02.md` §4, written the same morning, said FLUX 1.1
Pro Ultra's 4 megapixels was "the ceiling of the premium tier" and that 8K is
"eight times more pixels than the best model produces natively".
**That was wrong.** Two models generate **native 4K** today (§1):
Google's Nano Banana Pro at 4096×4096 (16.8 MP) and ByteDance's Seedream 4.0.
Against the best native output, 8K UHD (7680×4320, 33.2 MP) is about
**twice** the pixels, not eight times. The section in that file now points
here.

## 2. 8K: say the truth, as you wrote it

**Native 8K does not exist from anyone in this list.** Every 8K image on
offer anywhere is a 4K (or smaller) image enlarged by a second model.

| upscaler | what it costs — LOOKED UP 2026-10-02 | note |
|---|---|---|
| Topaz (official API) | $0.08–0.12 per credit; **one credit covers up to 24 MP** of output | 8K UHD is 33 MP, so it is past the first tier. The next tier's price was not read: **—** |
| Topaz via Replicate | $0.05 up to 24 MP … $0.82 up to 512 MP | same: 33 MP is in a tier not read, **—** |
| Magnific (Freepik API) | ~€0.20 for a 4× upscale; ~$0.32 for 6000×3500 (21 MP) | 8K is larger than 21 MP, so **≥ $0.32** |
| Real-ESRGAN | $0 + a GPU you run | open source |

**What upscaling does, in words a customer can check:** it invents the
detail. On an illustration that is an improvement. On a photograph of a
real shopfront, a real dish, a real face, it is a quiet fabrication. If 8K
is sold, it is sold as **"enlarged to 8K"**, never as "generated in 8K".

## 3. What it would cost — DERIVED

Credits = cost × 0.92 (USD→EUR) × margin ÷ the plan's credit rate, which is
the formula settlement uses (`src/lib/billing/credit-formula.ts`). Growth's
rate is EUR 0.0167, Ultimate's EUR 0.008 — which is why the same image is
more credits on Ultimate (see `docs/v6-pricing-2026-10-02.md` §3).

| image | cost | Growth at 5 (what the code applies) | Growth at 4 | Ultimate at 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Seedream 4.0, 4K | $0.03 | 9 | 7 | 18 |
| FLUX.2 [pro], 4 MP | $0.075 | 21 | 17 | 44 |
| Nano Banana Pro, 2K | $0.134 | 37 | 30 | 78 |
| FLUX.2 [max], 4 MP | $0.16 | 45 | 36 | 92 |
| **Nano Banana Pro, 4K** | **$0.24** | **67** | **53** | **138** |
| Seedream 4K → Magnific to 8K | ≥ $0.35 | ≥ 97 | ≥ 78 | ≥ 202 |
| **Nano Banana Pro 4K → Magnific to 8K** | **≥ $0.56** | **≥ 155** | **≥ 124** | **≥ 323** |

**Your ratio "2K: X · 4K: 3X · 8K: 10X" against what it actually costs**, on
one provider (Nano Banana Pro): 2K $0.134 → 4K $0.24 is **1.8×**; 4K → 8K
(+ Magnific) is ≥ $0.56, **≥ 4.2×** the 2K price. On Seedream, 2K and 4K cost
the same. So 3× and 10× are not the cost; they would be a price chosen above
the cost — which is a fine decision, but it is not "credits = 4× cost".

## 4. What it weighs — MEASURED

    node scripts/image-sizes.mjs

Three real photographs from images.unsplash.com, re-encoded the three ways
an image product stores them. **Measured 2026-10-02:**

| size | pixels | PNG | JPEG q90 | WebP q85 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2K | ~2.8 MP | 3.2–4.5 MB | 0.34–0.63 MB | 0.15–0.42 MB |
| 4K | ~9.8 MP | **10–16 MB** | 1.0–1.9 MB | 0.41–1.14 MB |
| 8K | ~39 MP | **30–62 MB** | 2.9–5.6 MB | 1.1–2.7 MB |

Generators return PNG. **Kept as PNG, one 8K image is 30–62 MB**, and Free's
whole storage (50 MB, `src/lib/files/limits.ts`) holds zero to one of them.
Converted to WebP at q85 the same picture is 1–3 MB. The format is the
decision, not the resolution: storing the original PNG costs ~20× the WebP.

DERIVED, at the 8K WebP upper bound (2.7 MB), how many fit in each plan's
storage if nothing else were stored: Free 18 · Starter 185 · Growth 758 ·
Professional 3,792 · Ultimate 18,962. As PNG at the upper bound (62 MB):
Free 0 · Starter 8 · Growth 33 · Professional 165 · Ultimate 825.

**What it cannot say:** what a GENERATED image weighs. A flat illustration
compresses far better than a photograph; the photographs here are the
heavy case, which is the one a quota has to survive.

## 5. What I would build, if you say build

| tier | what | why |
|---|---|---|
| Free | — no image generation | 50 MB holds one 8K PNG; the cost is per image and Free has no revenue |
| Starter | 2K (Seedream or FLUX.2 pro) | 7–21 credits, cheap enough to try |
| Growth | **4K native** (Seedream 4K at ~9, Nano Banana Pro 4K at ~67) | real 4K, nothing invented |
| Ultimate | **"enlarged to 8K"**, labelled as enlarged | ≥ 155 credits, and only where it is honest |

Every one of them shows its credits before the button — the rule
`scripts/tests/cost-before.test.mjs` now holds for every large action — and
stores **WebP by default**, the original PNG only on request and counted
against the quota.

**Cost-before is not optional here:** a Nano Banana Pro 4K → 8K image is
over the product's own large-action line (50 credits) on every plan.
