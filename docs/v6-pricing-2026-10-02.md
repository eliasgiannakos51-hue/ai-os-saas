## 1. The margin is already exactly 4x — on revenue, counting everything

    node scripts/tests/combined-ceiling.test.mjs    # section 1 prints it

**MEASURED 2026-10-02** — what an account can cost us at worst, against what
it pays, with credits AND the free chat every paid plan includes:

| plan | price | credits | free chat | total | real margin |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | EUR 20 | 20% | 5% | 25.0% | **4.00x** |
| Growth | EUR 50 | 20% | 5% | 25.0% | **4.00x** |
| Professional | EUR 100 | 20% | 5% | 25.0% | **4.00x** |
| Ultimate | EUR 200 | 20% | 5% | 25.0% | **4.00x** |

That is the 4x you asked for. The multiplier written in the code is **5 per
action** (`src/lib/billing/margin-policy.ts`, `PLAN_MARGIN_DEFAULTS`)
*because* credits are not the only cost: 1/5 of revenue goes to credits and
the remaining 5% to the free chat messages, and together they land on 25%.

**What "4 per action" would do instead — MEASURED, not argued.** With
`CREDIT_MARGIN_<PLAN>=4` on the four paid plans the real margin stays at
4.00x, because the code protects the ceiling — by taking the free chat away.
The monthly free messages go from **43 / 107 / 215 / 430 to 0** on Starter /
Growth / Professional / Ultimate. That is the "430" in your message: it is
what 4-per-action would delete.

So nothing was changed. **The gate now holds "exactly", from both sides:**

- `combined-ceiling.test.mjs` asserts each paid plan's real margin is
  **4.00x — not above, not below**. Before 2026-10-02 it was a ceiling only,
  so a price rise passed.
- `combined-ceiling.mutation.mjs`: Growth's 5 raised to 6 → red on
  *"growth: the real margin is exactly 4x"*; the free chat share cut from
  5% to 2% → red on all four plans. Both were green before.
- `margin-value.test.mjs` already held the per-action value at exactly 5 on
  paid plans and 6 on Free, and its sidecar already catches 5 → 4.

**If you still want 4 per action**, the decision is really "remove the free
chat messages from paid plans, or accept a real margin of about 3.3x". Say
which and it is a one-line change in each of two files, with the gates
updated in the same commit.

## 3. "500 / 430 / 100" — three different numbers, and one real inconsistency

**The three numbers are three different things.** They came from the
screenshots of 2026-10-02 (`scripts/ui-shots.mjs`):

- **500** in the header was the test account's made-up balance — 500 on an
  Ultimate account whose pricing card says 25,000. That was the screenshot
  harness's fault, and it is fixed: the harness now seeds the plan's own
  number from `src/lib/billing/plans.ts` and checks the header against it on
  every screen it shoots (*"header balance read on 6 screen(s); the plan
  grants 25000 — the same on every one"*). The phone header has no balance
  badge, so 6 is the desktop screens.
- **430** under the chat box is **free messages**, not credits — Ultimate's
  monthly free chat (section 1).
- **100** on the pricing page is the **Free plan's** credits.

**The balance itself has one source.** Every screen that shows it reads
`user_credits.credits_remaining`: the header and every component through
`src/components/credits/credits-context.tsx`, the settings page directly
(`src/app/dashboard/settings/page.tsx`), with the initial value from
`src/app/dashboard/layout.tsx`.

**The real inconsistency is that a credit is not the same size on every
plan.** The same website is **75 credits on Starter and 187 on Ultimate**.
That is deliberate — `src/lib/billing/credit-formula.ts` divides by each
plan's own price per credit (EUR 0.02 on Starter, EUR 0.008 on Ultimate),
because dividing by the list price is what once left Ultimate at a real
1.6x. But it means "25,000 credits" on Ultimate is not 25× "1,000 credits"
on Starter in what it buys: it is 10×, which is what the price says
(EUR 200 vs EUR 20).

**Two honest ways to make a credit one size everywhere** — a decision, not a
fix, so neither is made here:

| | what changes | effect |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Fewer credits, same size** | every credit is EUR 0.02 on every plan; Ultimate becomes **10,000** credits, Professional **5,000**, Growth **2,500** | a website is 75 credits on every plan; the margin is unchanged; the bulk discount disappears from the headline |
| **B. Same credits, same size** | the plans keep 25,000 / 10,000 / 3,000 and every credit is priced at EUR 0.02 | the headline stays, but credits on Ultimate then earn 5 × 0.008 / 0.02 = **2x** instead of 5x, and the real 4x is gone unless the price rises |

My recommendation is **A**: one credit means one thing, and the price
difference between plans is the number of credits, not their size.

## 4. Cost before every expensive feature — now a gate

    node scripts/tests/cost-before.test.mjs
    node scripts/tests/cost-before.mutation.mjs     # 7 of 7, measured 2026-10-02

"Expensive" is not a list anybody typed: it is every action the estimator
prices above the product's own large-action line (`LARGE_ACTION_CONFIRM_THRESHOLD`,
50) on any plan. Today that is Deep Research, the website, and the deep and
standard agent runs, and all four show their number before the button:

| action | where the number is | file |
|---|---|---|
| Website | "Estimated cost: N credits" beside the generate button | `src/components/website-builder/website-builder-workspace.tsx` |
| Deep Research | "Running this costs about N credits." under the research plan, before it runs | `src/components/research/research-workspace.tsx` |
| Agent runs | "N per run" beside every depth in the picker | `src/components/agents/depth-picker.tsx` |

The screens are listed by hand in `scripts/lib/cost-shown-before.mjs`,
because a scan for them found 11 of 30 actions and missed Deep Research and
the agent depths — the number reaches those screens from the server or
through a variable. The gate holds the list both ways: a new large action
with no entry is red, an entry for an action that no longer exists is red,
and the expression that renders the number must be in the file with the
comments stripped.

Mutations that go red (`cost-before.mutation.mjs`): Deep Research's estimate
removed, or moved into a comment; the website's estimate removed; the depth
picker's number replaced by a dash; a website edit grown until it is large
with no entry; the large-action line raised until nothing is large; a ghost
entry for an action that does not exist.
